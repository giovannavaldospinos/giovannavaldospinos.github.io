Example cases

Four areas of work. Company names are kept confidential.

Purchasing

Rising input costs due to logistics problems

What I did
I approved every purchase against 4 quotes and audited them by calling suppliers. I met with Purchasing and Production to replace products with cheaper alternatives, and recommended advance purchases when prices rose.
Outcome
Buying at the best price per product: about 10% below the average quote. Monthly cost variation of 1.6% to 2.2%, versus 7.2% in 2021.
Costing

Varieties that eroded profitability

What I did
Costing by variety, block and unit. Average margin of ~14%, cost per stem up to 2.5 times higher across varieties, and 1 in 4 lines with a negative margin (~21% of plants). I recommended removing those that eroded profitability.
Outcome
Sales wanted to keep them for the product mix; the analysis put numbers on that decision.
Inventory

Reconciling the plant inventory with the license records

What I did
Annual count by block and variety as an internal audit. Between 2022 and 2025 the total barely changed (−0.2%), with large differences by variety.
Outcome
When plants were missing from a variety that still sold well, they could be replanted using rights already paid for.
Planning

Coordinating sales, freight, staffing and inputs before peak season

What I did
Stem projection by variety based on pinched plants, minus 15% for domestic sales. Reds were about a third.
Outcome
Basis for negotiating freight with cargo agencies, pre-selling to customers, planning staff and inputs ahead of time, and avoiding shortages in peak season.
